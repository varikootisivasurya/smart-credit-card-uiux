# Smart Credit Card Recommender Platform – UI/UX

A mobile-first UI/UX design that helps users discover the best credit cards
based on their spending habits, lifestyle, and credit profile.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal
To simplify credit card selection by providing clear recommendations,
transparent rewards estimation, and easy comparison in a clean, modern UI.

---

## 🧠 UX Principles Followed
<ul>
  <li>Simple & clean interface</li>
  <li>Mobile-first responsive design</li>
  <li>Minimal steps to recommendations</li>
  <li>Visual comparison & savings clarity</li>
  <li>Trust-building through transparency</li>
</ul>
